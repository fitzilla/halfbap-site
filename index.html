About

Making records since 2011

Opened by producers Graham Davidson and George Sloan, Half Bap sits on the cobbled streets of Commercial Court in Belfast city centre, right beside the Duke of York.

We don't just press record. We work on arrangements, sounds and performances with you, across a 500 sq ft live room, a dead room and a big collection of vintage and modern gear.

Producer / Engineer

Graham Davidson

Multi award-winning producer with 20 years in music. Known for alt rock, punk and indie bands that sound massive and still sound like themselves.

Producer / Engineer

George Sloan

Working with artists from New York to Indonesia since 2009, across bands, theatre and dance. Heard on RTÉ Radio 1, BBC Radio 2 and 6 Music.

Producer / Engineer

Kacper Lewandoski

Award-winning heavy metal producer and mix engineer. Extremely fast, or slow and low-tuned: both welcome.

Services

Every stage of your record, under one roof

New artist or already established, on vintage and modern gear, with producers and engineers who work closely with you throughout.

  1. 01

    Recording & Production

    Solo takes to full-band live sessions across the live room, vocal booth and control room.

  2. 02

    Mixing & Mastering

    Tracked here, at home or elsewhere. Digital precision with high-end analogue outboard.

  3. 03

    Voice Overs

    A fully treated vocal booth and plenty of mics for TV, film and theatre.

  4. 04

    Dry Hire

    Hire the studio and run your own sessions.

  5. 05

    Sound Design

    Soundscapes, foley and music for screen and stage.

  6. 06

    Remote Recording

    We bring the studio rig to your venue to record live.

Debut album Fine · Out now

Alana Henderson

“Not Going Anywhere”

Alana Henderson recorded her new single “Not Going Anywhere” here at Half Bap. It was the first look at Fine, her long-awaited debut album, out now.

A song about a fresh start that became an ending, built around Alana’s cello and acoustic guitar with George Sloan, Katie Richardson and Sean Doone. The video, made with Jason Martin, follows her through the mist on Belfast’s Black Mountain.
